Final Design Overview

Key Screens of the App

Home Screen

Shows bin locations, collection alerts, and eco-friendly tips.

Map Screen

Interactive map displaying bin fill levels with color coding for quick status checking.

Rewards Page

Encourages sustainable behavior with redeemable points and achievements.

Impact of Design

  • Cleaner Neighborhoods: Real-time alerts reduce overflowing bins.
  • Increased Recycling Awareness: Educational tips improve sorting practices.
  • Optimized Routes for Municipality: Data-driven insights save time and resources.
  • Enhanced Engagement: Reward incentives encourage responsible waste habits.

Conclusion

Key Takeaways from the Design Thinking Process

  • Empathy is essential to understanding the varied needs of residents, workers, and companies.
  • Iterative feedback and testing led to a more user-friendly and impactful app.
  • Data-driven design creates practical solutions for urban waste management.
  • Providing incentives fosters positive user engagement and sustainability.

Future Improvements

Potential enhancements could include integrating AI for predictive bin fill levels, expanding reward options, and adding community engagement features like waste tracking and shared goals.
