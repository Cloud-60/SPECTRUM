Empathize & Define

Empathize: Understanding the Users

Resident

John: A busy professional who wants a clean neighborhood but has limited time to engage in waste management.

Municipality Worker

Maria: A waste collector who needs efficient routes and communication about bin fill levels.

Recycling Company Employee

Amit: Focused on maximizing recyclables but lacks data on residents' waste habits.

Pain Points

  • Overflowing bins due to irregular collection or public misuse
  • Recycling challenges from low awareness about proper recycling practices
  • Miscommunication on bin status or collection schedules
  • Lack of incentives for correct disposal and recycling

Problem Statement

"How might we make waste management more efficient, informative, and rewarding for all stakeholders?"

Goals of the App

  • Efficient Waste Disposal: Provide real-time alerts to avoid overflow
  • Enhanced Recycling: Educate users on proper sorting
  • Data-Driven Insights: Optimize routes for waste collection
  • Incentives for Users: Rewards for sustainable behaviors
